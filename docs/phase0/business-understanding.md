# Phase 0: Business Understanding

## 1. Real banking problem
Banks face a core challenge: deciding whether a customer is likely to repay a loan. If the bank approves too many risky customers, default losses rise. If it rejects too many good customers, revenue and market share fall.

This project addresses the problem by building an explainable risk assessment platform that estimates default probability and supports human decision-making.

## 2. Why credit risk assessment matters
Credit risk assessment is important because it directly affects:
- financial losses
- loan approval efficiency
- regulatory compliance
- customer experience
- portfolio performance

## 3. Main stakeholders
- Credit analysts: review risk scores and investigate borderline cases
- Risk managers: monitor portfolio risk and define acceptable thresholds
- Banking operations teams: handle application workflow and customer communication
- Business leaders: need portfolio insights and high-level risk trends

## 4. Business objective
Predict the probability of default and provide explainable, actionable recommendations for lenders.

## 5. Business KPIs
- Default rate
- High-risk customer percentage
- Approval efficiency
- False negative rate for risky customers
- Portfolio risk concentration
- Analyst review time

## 6. Business value created
This project creates value by helping banks:
- detect risky applicants earlier
- reduce default losses
- improve consistency across decisions
- support analysts with transparent reasons for each score
- turn historical credit data into actionable intelligence
